## Key positions

Thirteen cities founded on landmarks at the start. They cannot be razed. At the end of turn 38 (9 PM, June 8) the Allies win **Hold the Beachhead** if they hold at least 10 of them; otherwise Germany wins **Contain the Invasion**. Historically the Allies held 10 by then. The five beaches are also where the follow-up waves come ashore once they are in Allied hands.
